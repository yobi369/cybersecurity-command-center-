## Security and privacy impact
Threats, data classification, secrets, authorization, audit, privacy, compliance questions and mitigations.

## Operational impact
Deployment, monitoring, support, backup, recovery, migration, performance and cost.

## Consequences
Positive, negative and neutral consequences. Include migration and rollback.

## Verification
Tests, evidence references, reviewer and acceptance criteria.

## Change history
Record amendments and superseding ADRs; do not erase historical decisions.
